Initial execution protocol — 2026-11-23

Research question
Can the current residual guide kernel-column replacements that reduce the total
time to reach a prescribed solution accuracy, while keeping the number of
retained columns fixed?

Scope
The submitted proposal and docs/research-plan.txt define the full project.
This execution implements the initial method and its planned controls, checks
numerical correctness, then runs a bounded diagnostic and an initial performance
comparison. The first configurations are subsets of the full rank,
regularization, target, and checkpoint study; their evidence does not replace it.
The method can change in response to observed results. Keep the research question
and the complete cost comparison when revising the mechanism.

Numerical method
K_ij = exp(-||x_i-x_j||^2/(2h^2)); A = K + lambda I.
S has exactly k unique indices. C = K[:,S], W = K[S,S].
M = C W^dagger C^T + lambda I.
RPCholesky selects the initial S using its residual diagonal.
At a checkpoint, r = y - A alpha is explicitly recomputed. For each of four
sequential candidates, sample an unselected index proportionally to r_j^2;
consider removing each index from S union {j}, including the no-op removal of j.
Accept only a stable decrease greater than 1e-12 relative in q(S;r)=r^T M^-1 r.
Keep r fixed throughout that checkpoint's sequential exchanges.
Restart once if at least one exchange is accepted; otherwise continue PCG.
No iteration changes the system matrix, target, or solution being sought.

The small all-removal identity uses a cached C^T C and costs O(nk+k^3) per
candidate. The first cache construction costs O(nk^2) and is charged to the first
exchange update; fixed and matched-restart methods never construct this cache.
Accepted preconditioners are rebuilt in O(nk^2+k^3). Singular or
unreliable scoring systems fall back to direct stable evaluation of all removals;
this slower path costs O(nk^3+k^4) and is counted in numerical_fallbacks.
Small eigenvalues are truncated according to the threshold in numerics.py.
Exactly k columns are retained even when their numerical rank is smaller.

Data and pairing
Concrete Compressive Strength: 1030 observations, 8 features, UCI.
Airfoil Self-Noise: 1503 observations, 5 features, UCI.
Official archives, member names, licenses, and hashes are in data/manifest.json.
Synthetic: 640 observations, 8 features, four Gaussian clusters with probabilities
0.50, 0.25, 0.15, 0.10; center standard deviation 1.5, within-cluster 0.45.
Smooth y = sin(x_0) + 0.3 x_1^2 - 0.2 x_2 + 0.05 Gaussian noise.
Random y is independent standard Gaussian noise. All synthetic targets use the
same inputs and train/test split for each seed. The mixed target combines two
orthogonal bands of the training-kernel eigenvectors: descending zero-based
indices 1:9 and max(16,n_train//8):max(16,n_train//8)+8. Each component has zero
training mean and squared norm n_train/2. Independent seeded coefficients are
projected onto the zero-mean subspace of each band and normalized. Held-out
targets extend the same kernel eigenfunctions from training inputs. Held-out
inputs do not determine the bands, coefficients, scaling, or bandwidth.
The eigendecomposition constructs synthetic data offline; no eigenvectors are
stored in the solver input. The saved target_construction metadata identifies
the bands and coefficient seed. Development seeds0/1 have been prepared; the
revision's confirmation seeds5--9 remain unused.
Use 80% training, 20% held-out data. Fit feature and target standardization only
on training observations. h is the median training pairwise distance.
lambda is the coefficient in K + lambda I, with no hidden multiplication by n.
Seeds 0 through 4 independently control data/split, initial pivots, and candidate
draws via separate SeedSequence streams. All Nyström methods use the same initial
selected indices for a given case and rank.

Methods implemented
cg: unpreconditioned conjugate gradients.
fixed: PCG with the initial RPCholesky Nyström preconditioner.
residual_exchange: the submitted residual-guided exchange method.
matched_restart: the fixed preconditioner, restarted at adaptive accepted-update
iterations that occur before this control itself converges.
target_exchange: y supplies both sampling probabilities and exchange scores.
uniform_exchange: uniform candidates with the current residual supplying scores.
periodic_rebuild: recompute a rank-k RPCholesky preconditioner at each checkpoint.
lbfgs: inverse L-BFGS with at most floor(k/2) block secant pairs (s, A s).
Pairs and the inverse are frozen within each PCG block; a valid pair is added at
the checkpoint, followed by restart. Initial scale is 1/(1+lambda); subsequent
two-loop scaling is s^T A s / ||A s||^2. This specifies the comparator completely.
The secant-pair vector count is controlled; its total storage is not exactly equal
to the Nyström implementation, which also retains its orthonormal factor basis.
Dense Cholesky supplies an independent accuracy reference outside timed workers.

Correctness before timing
Compare inverse applications and all-removal scores with independent dense
calculations, including dependent columns. Check SPD solver solutions against
direct solves, verify restart rules, and check L-BFGS against explicit inverse
BFGS updates. Numerical tests validate implementation, not research performance.

Mechanism diagnostic
configs/diagnostic.json fixes five seeds, two targets, lambda in {1e-3,1e-5},
k=16, checkpoint=20, four candidates, and at most the first three accepted
checkpoints per solve. From exactly the same alpha, restart separate old-M and
new-M branches and freeze each preconditioner for 5, 10, and 20 steps.
Measure q_new/q_old, actual A-energy error, true residual, and coefficient error
against a Cholesky solution. If a branch stops at its numerical floor before the
horizon, record its actual step count. These diagnostic runs are excluded from
performance measurements. Bound wall time at 300 seconds, checking between cases.
Checkpoint observations within a solve are dependent; do not treat them as
independent samples in significance tests.

Performance measurements
Use float64, CPU only, one BLAS thread, and identical 128-row blockwise kernel
matvecs. Timed workers never form a dense n-by-n kernel. Every method verifies
the true relative residual at each checkpoint and before accepting convergence.
Tolerance 1e-8, maximum 2000 iterations. A failed solve remains a failed solve.
Total solve time includes RPCholesky setup, preconditioner application, candidate
selection, fallback scoring, accepted factor rebuilds, recurrence restarts,
kernel matvecs, and residual verification. Separate phase times and operation
counts accompany each result. Downloading, preprocessing, bandwidth selection,
dense reference preparation, process startup, and post-solve accuracy checks are
outside this common timing boundary.
An untimed adaptive calibration records the matched-restart schedule for each
case/rank. Timed methods are shuffled per repetition. Timed adaptive runs must
reproduce the calibrated schedule exactly. Calibration work is recorded apart
from the solver times. Timing repetitions use the same random choices; distinct
seeds provide distinct cases. Use the median of three repeats in the first
multi-seed comparison. One-repeat pilot measurements are execution/cost checks.
Store process peak RSS through the end of the solve, including interpreter,
libraries, and inputs. The separate max_preconditioner_state_bytes is the largest
single retained state, not peak temporary workspace. Accepted exchanges may
temporarily hold both states; actual process RSS includes those allocations.
Peak RSS increments can be zero when imports already established a larger peak.
Store solution difference, prediction difference, and held-out RMSE after timing.

Execution limits and records
The pilot allows 300 wall seconds and 60 seconds per worker. The initial campaign
uses configs/initial.json: four workloads, five seeds, k=16, lambda=1e-3,
three timing repeats, and cg/fixed/residual_exchange/matched_restart. Its maximum
wall time is 900 seconds with 60 seconds per worker. It preserves every completed
record if it exhausts that limit. The other four implemented variants are run
in the one-seed pilot; their multi-seed comparison remains part of the full plan.
No GPU use, cloud jobs, external publishing, or
unbounded search. Source hashes and all settings are saved for every run group.
Campaign outputs are immutable: reruns use new directories. Do not edit source
during a timing campaign. Keep timeouts, nonconvergence, and failures in the
records; compare time-to-accuracy only when the paired methods both converge.
In summary.csv, median_seconds is the duration of all attempts, including
nonconverged ones; median_time_to_accuracy_seconds is populated only when every
repetition converged. Use the latter for successful solve-time comparisons.

Interpretation and next steps
A smaller q at an unchanged alpha does not reduce the actual error. The paired
branch diagnostic tests whether the next iterations benefit. If branches improve
but total time worsens, inspect update cost and restart frequency. If branches do
not improve, revisit the score or retention strategy before optimizing updates.
Uniform and y-based controls distinguish candidate sampling from dependence on
the current solve. Matched restarts distinguish column changes from restarting.
Do not select only successful datasets or relabel a narrower setting as the
project's contribution. Preserve the original question and report all cases.
After this initial evidence, select the next mechanism experiment, then extend
the planned rank/regularization/frequency and target studies.
